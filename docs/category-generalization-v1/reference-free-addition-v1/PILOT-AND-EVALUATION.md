Every runtime context is None. Ground truth is separate offline metadata computed with exact Fraction arithmetic, not copied from Rule, a model, verifier agreement or runtime context. These are diagnostic development examples, not held-out cases or an empirical policy dataset. Further overlap/custody review against all historical source families is required before study admission; exact unseen wording alone does not prove independence.

A full feasible-team diagnostic evaluates the three eligible actual verifiers once per candidate: **12 attempts** (4 Rule, 4 Evidence, 4 Confidence), at most **20 Ollama generations** at existing n=5, at most **80** local version/tags metadata GETs, **1800 seconds** outer wall time, no warmup/repeat/retry. Eight team/aggregation strategies can then be replayed under the complete original finalizer on authorized fresh recordings, without fitting. This is the smallest paired correct/incorrect, carrying/cancellation diagnostic that includes every planning-eligible verifier. It is not a statistically sufficient fitting or certification sample. If all required resource bindings cannot be supplied, stop; do not silently restrict the experiment to force Rule-only.

The twenty existing 60-second generation timeouts alone total at most 1200 seconds; the outer 1800-second limit takes precedence. CPU-only local resources, no paid service or asserted monetary price. Existing localhost Ollama/model digest/prompt/sampling, exact corpus/index/receipt and embedding/NLI identities must be attested, unchanged. No automatic Ollama startup, model download, index rebuild, package install or service update. No child processes. Stop on first safety/provenance mismatch, deadline, unexpected native name, generation/network/write ceiling, ambiguous journal or need for an unapproved dependency. Record factual abstention or legitimate uncertainty as observed outcomes; do not fix them by changing labels or thresholds.

## New empirical study — staged preparation, no fitting now

The earlier proposed 32 history + 32 development + 64 held-out groups (256 paired candidates, up to 768 three-verifier attempts and 1280 generations) remains a non-authorized pilot-scale design, not a run request or acceptance-size justification. No held-out label file was generated or accessed in this checkpoint. Prepare independently custodied datasets only after scope, sampling, attacks, resource lock and exact budgets are reviewed.

1. **History:** independent operand-pair families, with commutative/order and wording variants grouped. Include positive/negative/zero/repeated operands, carrying, cancellation and contract boundaries. Use independent integer/Fraction labels. Estimate attempt/startup costs separately and derive reputations/dependencies with existing history-only calibration. Freeze snapshot/resource IDs, priors, penalties, budget selection protocol, contributor/coverage/threshold semantics and sampling decisions before development evaluation. Historical comparison costs in the source audit are not new measurements.
2. **Development:** disjoint families with correct/incorrect pairs, accepted new wordings and independently labelled plausible arithmetic errors. Include predefined failure/abstention, missing resource, unresolved coverage, zero-influence and insufficient-contributor fixtures; do not disguise unavailable attempts as successful reports. Add malformed answers, unsupported operations, multiple expressions and out-of-bounds inputs as routing-contract tests, not ordinary factual rejects. Ground-truth labels never enter a live ValidationRequest or reference.
3. **Untouched reservation:** a different custodian seals group identities and label/outcome commitments; no held-out outcome influences sampling, costs, attacks, thresholds, baseline choice or fitting. Check source/semantic family overlap and record that procedural reservation is not independent blinding. Final authorization must specify exact reserved IDs, frozen model/source revision, setup and policy freeze. Existing held-out collection gates in `collection.py` and final scoring/readiness gates remain authoritative.
4. **Payoffs/fitting:** enumerate all genuinely feasible rows at the frozen history-derived budget using the current enumerator. Resource-blocked teams remain blocked, not assigned invented payoffs. Collect complete approved raw reports and actual resource observations. Use existing replay/finalization, loss/attacker/cost/coverage definitions and `construct_payoffs`; retain the entire feasible strategy space in minimax fitting. If reliability-first/coverage constraints are part of the preregistration, keep their complete empirical binding and solver certificate. Probability one is valid if it results naturally. No manual favorable weights or forced mixing.
5. **Attack protocol:** freeze clean and declared dependency attack columns before fitting. Use symmetric per-verifier and correlated failure/abstention/perturbation rules under an explicit threat model, including shared model/service failures where supported. Do not choose attacks after seeing which verifier wins. Preserve negative results and cost/infeasibility findings.
6. **Baselines:** use `app/game/research/comparison.py`/readiness where compatible: captured greedy selection, every feasible individual verifier (only Rule independently covers arithmetic here), best development-selected fixed team/aggregation, uniform mixture over every feasible strategy, fitted minimax and historical fitted policies only under genuinely matching contracts. The reference-present policy is not a valid reference-free performance baseline. Evaluate greedy under the same budget, resources, contributor/coverage and complete-team failure semantics; an infeasible greedy plan is an explicit finding, not silently repaired or given free calls. Current historical comparison support must be extended in owned adapters only where contract-compatible.
7. **Acceptance:** predeclare sample size, false-accept bound/confidence level and numerical gates before collection. Report correct accepts/rejects, false accepts/rejects, uncertainty/unresolved rate, decision coverage, selective risk among decided outcomes, achieved requirement coverage, weighted decision loss, per-verifier/end-to-end/startup latencies, actual attempt/generation/service costs and grouped uncertainty intervals. Stratify untouched question families and declared attacks. Zero observed errors does not prove zero population risk; none of the proposed group counts alone supports universal correctness or optimality.

## Other supported categories

Factual diagnostics must use the original frozen corpus and independent source-snapshot labels, including supported/absent/contradictory facts and unavailable resources. Retrieval success is not truth. Missing corpus facts should remain uncertain when no evidence supports a qualified rejection. Do not claim web coverage or replace mismatched corpus bytes. Existing reference-present factual policy may serve unseen compatible questions; a different reference/task/resource contract needs its own proof or fit.

Semantic diagnostics require legitimate independent reference material and pin original thresholds/model/mode. Reserve novel equivalence, numeric contradiction, negation, entity substitution and relation-change families independently. Similarity can miss contradictions; record that negative finding rather than editing the verifier or relabelling truth. Fictional frames measure equivalence, not world-fact knowledge.

Confidence diagnoses completion/consistency under the actual provider/model/sampling contract. Include wrong-but-consistent and unstable/no-majority cases. Keep v5 consistency assessment separate; it requires its own compatible empirical artifact before fitted use and cannot establish factual evidence. Combined tasks require actual obligation/component binding and all selected resources; up to four verifiers is a capability-constrained possibility, not an entitlement to every subset.
