## What selection means today

`question → question-only analyzer → task/domain/requirement/difficulty/reference
signature → explicit category/budget registry → compatible frozen policy → one
sampled strategy → complete actual selected calls → finalized decision`.

[`TaskQuestionAnalyzer`](../app/analysis/task_analyzer.py) preserves existing atomic
rules. [`CapabilityProbeAnalyzer`](../app/analysis/capability_probe_analyzer.py)
adds only explicitly versioned diagnostics. Compound classification uses the
finite source-descriptor catalog in [`component_sources.py`](../app/game/component_sources.py);
its signature includes two required checks and catalog identity. References have
an explicit opt-in [`bounded_reference_frames_v1`](../app/analysis/reference_study_analyzer.py)
category: a constant easy/.1 score, fixed before outputs, replaces accidental
word-count score differences **for that explicitly allowed study only**. It is
not a measured difficulty estimate or a change to the default analyzer.

[`TaskPolicyRouter`](../app/game/task_conditioning.py) and the owned
[`QuestionPolicyRouter`](../app/game/question_routing.py) select compatible
registrations. They do not fit on an incoming answer or use answer correctness,
verifier reports, test outcomes or reputation updates to invent a category.
Within one supported signature and budget, every question receives the same
strategy distribution. RNG sampling within that distribution is randomness,
not further question adaptation. Atomic arithmetic and factual policies choose
Rule alone and Evidence alone respectively with probability one. Compound selects
the Evidence/Rule pair because both independent *requirements* must be covered;
this does not imply independently failing implementations.

The model supports enumerating team sizes 1–4. It does not promise a different
size for each question, force pairs/triples to occur, or treat more names as more
evidence. No real empirical policy selecting a triple has been demonstrated.
Synthetic fixtures that exercise sizes three/four remain software tests only.

## Scope, grammar and binding

The owned study registry uses an explicit allowlist, separately from the untouched
production prototype guard. Loadable artifacts do not authorize arbitrary inputs.
Atomic policies cover their original narrow addition and pinned factual cohorts.
New difference wording, general reasoning, unsupported compound operations,
missing references and unknown categories must fail closed rather than borrow a
similar policy. The source-known compound grammar is:

```text
What is the area of <catalog entity>, and what is that area plus <decimal> square kilometres? Give both values.
What is the population of <catalog entity>, and what is that population plus <decimal>? Give both values.
```

The parser accepts only its finite bounded grammar and catalog. The acceptance
registry further restricts it to exact study questions. Compound answers must be
an object with exactly two **decimal strings**:

```json
{"base":"765","derived":"775"}
```

The above explains format only; Bahrain is excluded from the fresh empirical
study because it was already probed. Duplicate, missing or extra fields, numeric
JSON values and unsupported input fail answer binding. The fact check sees a
bound factual question and submitted base value. Rule sees the submitted base
plus increment and the submitted derived value. No gold base is substituted. A
wrong base with internally correct arithmetic therefore needs the factual check;
passing arithmetic cannot certify the whole answer.

Reference questions are `Paraphrase this reference: <exact fixed reference>` with
that legitimate text supplied as context. Only the 30 predeclared questions are
allowed by the study registry. References never change after verifier outcomes.
The existing Confidence provider receives its established question/answer inputs;
prose context is not a list of externally supplied judgment labels. Retrieval
corpus contents remain unchanged; these constructed frames are not indexed facts.

## Execution, contributors and cost

[`component_execution.py`](../app/game/component_execution.py) binds and samples
one complete joint plan, scopes the selected native implementations and runs every
scheduled call. [`execution.py`](../app/game/execution.py) and
[`finalization.py`](../app/game/finalization.py) share runtime/replay public-decision
semantics. Each component must satisfy its output coverage, informative history
influence and .55 threshold. All components passing accepts the whole answer;
any qualified component rejection rejects it; otherwise it is uncertain. Execution
or provenance failures retain their distinct blocking reasons.

Selected names, returned reports, achieved capabilities and informative contributors
are distinct audit fields. Semantic cosine coverage alone is not a logical
equivalence certificate or informative corroboration. A history mean of .5 gives
zero log-odds influence. Shared dependency aggregation limits duplicated influence.
History weights are task-specific, frozen before test, and are not answer-correctness
probabilities. No held-out reputation update occurs.

Compound schedules exactly one Evidence fact call and one Rule calculation call
per answer. Cost sums **every call**, even if a future plan repeats the same name
on different components; distinct-name count is not the billable/timing count.
Current compound cost estimates are E 84.070561 ms and R .122528 ms, budget
84.193090 ms, derived only from existing history/development calls. Reference
costs use its measured history/development per-verifier means and sum-roster budget.
Initialization and resource-observation overhead are excluded from verifier timers;
these are engineering measurements, not prices or end-to-end latency guarantees.
Test timing is reported separately and never recalibrates frozen costs.

No game-mode early stopping uses a favorable prefix or chooses another team after
seeing reports. The unrelated baseline adaptive pipeline remains its owner's
responsibility. Fixed and existing greedy comparisons must use matching contracts,
history, costs, coverage and complete selected calls; the greedy initial selection
is not evidence for an independently defined compound greedy executor.

## Experimental limits and publication boundary

The dependency registry connects Evidence/Semantic through embeddings and
Evidence/Confidence through NLI; unresolved risks put all four names in a shared
unknown failure group. Report-corruption attacks may therefore corrupt all
selected names together, including both compound components. Different verifier
names are not independent failure paths. Forced-support vulnerability remains;
these are simulated report-level attacks, not observed upstream compromises.

Arithmetic/factual singleton equivalence to their best fixed baselines and
compound pair equivalence are reported honestly. Larger teams need not improve
the primary objective, coverage or cost. This source-known numeric/factual and
short constructed-reference evidence cannot establish broad domain/difficulty
adaptation, unrestricted compound support or a minimax advantage over fixed teams.
Small-cluster bootstrap intervals describe the chosen cohort; degenerate intervals
at zero observed errors do not prove zero population risk.

Publication must exclude virtual environments, model caches/weights, index
directories, Ollama files, DB/user data, credentials and unrelated original-checkout
changes. In particular, `artifacts/compound-study-36-v1/local-custody/` contains an
ignored original-execution source archive including unrelated owner work: **do not
publish it**. Preserve it locally and retain its provenance record, without
claiming the owned source alone reproduces that full application digest.
Historical local paths in audit artifacts are retained intentionally and should
be reviewed for identity disclosure before public release; do not rewrite frozen
history. No commit, push, merge or activation has been performed.
